Welcome to KCC

School Philosophy

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc. is committed to forming socially conscious, responsible, and service-oriented individuals, guided by the teachings of Christ. They embody a profound understanding of the challenges and opportunities inherent in a rapidly evolving world. As an evangelizing arm of the Diocese of Kabankalan, KCC seeks to proclaim and spread the teachings of the Church. The institution shepherds students and the community toward a deeper relationship with christ; strengthening faith that transforms lives through service, compassion, and steadfast devotion to Christian values. As a preferential option for the poor, Kabankalan Catholic College affirms that education is a universal right. Rooted in a curriculum that integrates the arts, sciences, and the Gospel, a KCC education fosters well-rounded development--one that nurtures each person's mind, body, and soul within a safe and supportive environment. As an educational institution, KCC seeks academic excellence with a strong moral foundation characterized by integrity, compassion, and generosity. Our students and graduates are equipped with skills, knowledge, and disposition necessary to contribute positively to society and to respond effectively to the needs of the Diocese, the nation, and the global community. KCC aims to achieve all these through the delivery of exceptional instruction; the development of suitable learning spaces and facilities; the conduct of innovative research and creative projects; and the engagement in strong and purposeful outreach.

Vission

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nattion, and the world.

Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagement, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.
